Coinsy Finance

Personal finance tracker

Reads bank and UPI statements from five banks, sorts spending with batched LLM calls plus a rule-based fallback for when the model isn't sure, and forecasts next month's spend by category with linear regression. 5–6 classmates use it and found it more useful than other finance apps they'd tried.
